About me

I'm a master's student at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS), enrolled at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). I am advised by Guojun Yuan, a senior engineer at the High-Performance Computing Center. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Information Security from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).

At ICT, I contribute to the development of AI for Science (AI4S) accelerator chips for molecular dynamics simulations, with responsibility for verification and contributions to parts of the design. Across my research internships, I have worked on LLM agent framework design, implementation, and training; dataset construction for evaluating agent capabilities; and pretraining data processing and algorithms.

My current research interests center on data science for LLM training, including data mixtures, data quality, data utilization and training efficiency, and the effects of training on the same data over multiple epochs. I am also interested in post-training algorithms and data construction for post-training long-horizon agents.

Current research interests

  • Data science for LLM training
  • Post-training algorithms
  • Long-horizon agent data

    WaveCC

    Credit-Guided Congestion Control on Wafer-Scale On-Chip Networks for Molecular Dynamics

    Tao Jiang, Shixiong Qi, Zhan Wang, Ning Kang, Fan Yang, Yuanzhe Wang, Zejun Li, Guanglei Chen, Jing Xu, Wenzhe Li, Guangming Tan, Guojun Yuan

    Credit-guided congestion control for molecular-dynamics workloads on wafer-scale AI4S chips. WaveCC adapts communication stride and per-PE injection rates using router credits, without changing router microarchitecture.

    My contribution

    Collected baseline measurements and packet-level traces with BookSim2 and LAMMPS to evaluate credit-guided congestion control for molecular-dynamics workloads on wafer-scale chips.
